What we do

Aeration and overseeding, done right

Front, side, or back yard — one yard or the whole property.

Core Lawn Aeration

Pulls small soil plugs across the lawn so air, water, and nutrients can reach the roots. Front, side, or back yard — you choose the area.

Aeration + Overseeding

Seed goes down while the soil is open, so it lands in the plug holes where it germinates best. We use a professional-grade Tall Fescue blend with zero weed seed.

Why aerate

Compacted soil starves a lawn

Foot traffic, mowing, and clay soil press the ground tight over the years. Aeration opens it back up.

  • Better root access. Open channels let oxygen, water, and fertilizer get down where roots actually are.
  • Seed that takes. Overseeding into fresh plug holes gives new grass much better seed-to-soil contact than seed thrown on top.
  • Right time of year. Cool-season lawns like tall fescue do best when aerated and seeded in early fall — call for scheduling.
